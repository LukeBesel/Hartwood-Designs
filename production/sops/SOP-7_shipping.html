SOP7

Shipping

When: Every packed kit, once a dayTime: About 2 min per kit (Estimate)Who: Packer
You need:Planner tabletShop app (Shopify or Etsy)Shipping scale4 x 6 in label printerPacking tape
1
Open the Packed column in the planner.
Ship the oldest orders first.
Every packed kit has an order.
2
Match the box to the order.
Kit name on the order = kit name on the lid label.
Gift order? No price slip in the box.
3
Close and tape the box.
One strip across the opening edge.
Sheets do not slide when you tilt it.
4
Weigh it and buy the label.
In the shop app, USPS Ground Advantage. Round the weight up.
Address matches the order.
5
Stick the label on the bottom.
Not over the info label. The lid stays clean, because many kits are gifts.
Barcode flat, no wrinkles.
6
Mark it Shipped in the planner.
The shop app emails the tracking number to the customer.
Packed column is empty.
7
Hand over to USPS.
Book a free USPS Package Pickup on usps.com (check that it takes Ground Advantage), or drop off at the post office.
Keep the scan receipt until delivered.

Hold the box if

  • The order changed or was cancelled.
  • The address looks incomplete (no apartment number, no ZIP).
  • The box is crushed or a corner is open: repack it.
