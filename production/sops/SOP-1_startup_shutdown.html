SOP1

Start-up and shut-down

When: Every day you cutTime: 10 min to start, 10 min to close (Estimate)Who: Whoever runs the laser that day
You need:Room thermometerLens tissue and lens cleanerBrush and binPlanner tablet

Start of the day

1
Check the room temperature.
The water loop must stay above 10 °C (50 °F). Cold, damp wood also fits differently.
Below 10 °C: do not start. Warm the room first.
2
Clear around the laser.
Nothing on the lid. No paper, offcuts or rags next to the machine.
CO2 extinguisher and fire blanket within reach.
3
Open the vent. Switch on the exhaust.
Run it before, during and after every job.
A tissue held at the intake gets pulled in.
4
Look at the cooling water.
Clear, between MIN and MAX, no bubbles in the tube.
Low or cloudy: top up with distilled water (SOP-5).
5
Turn on the laser.
Wait until the head moves home and stops.
No error on the control panel.
6
Open LightBurn and connect.
Use the saved device profile. Do not change layer settings by hand.
Status shows Ready.
7
Look at the lens.
A smoke film or dots on the lens make weak cuts. Wipe with lens tissue and cleaner.
Clean, no cracks, no pits.

Stop and fix first if

  • Smoke smell in the room while a job runs: the exhaust is not pulling.
  • The water is warm, low, or has bubbles in the tube.
  • The control panel shows an error, or the head does not home.

End of the day

8
Let the smoke clear.
Leave the exhaust on for 5 minutes after the last job.
No smoke smell when you lift the lid.
9
Empty the crumb tray. Sweep the bed.
Small scraps are fuel for a fire.
Tray empty, bed clear, bin emptied outside.
10
Switch off: laser first, then exhaust.
Close the window vent.
Winter: the room stays above 10 °C overnight, or drain the water loop.
11
Log the day in the planner.
Mark the batches you cut. Log any problem on the Quality tab.
Every batch cut today shows Cut.

Hartwood Designs, SOP-1, Rev A, 2026-10-12. Times and settings marked Estimate are planning numbers until measured on the real machine. Machine details follow the OMTech manual when it differs from this sheet.
