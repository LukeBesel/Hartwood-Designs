SOP6

Fire and emergency

When: Any flame that does not go out, smoke, water leak or injury. Read it before you first use the laser, and practice it.Time: Seconds countWho: Everyone in the workshop
You need:CO2 extinguisher at the laser (5 lb, B:C)Fire blanketPhoneSmoke alarmFirst aid kit

Flame in the laser

1
Press Pause or Stop.
This stops the beam. If the air assist keeps blowing, switch it off.
Do not open the lid.
2
Keep the lid shut.
Without fresh air most small flames die out in seconds.
Watch through the window.
3
Still burning after 10 seconds, or growing?
Switch off the laser at the power switch. Get the extinguisher. (10 s is an Estimate.)
Exhaust stays on only if smoke is going outside.
4
Use the CO2 extinguisher.
Pull the pin. Open the lid a little. Aim at the base of the flame. Squeeze in short bursts.
CO2 leaves no powder in the machine.
5
A burning sheet outside the machine?
Lay the fire blanket over it. Leave it until it is cold.
Never carry a burning sheet.
6
Cannot stop it? Get out.
Close the door behind you. Call 911 from outside. Do not go back in.
Your safety first. The laser can be replaced.
7
After any flame: watch for 30 minutes.
Embers can relight. Check the lens and bed before the next job.
Log it on the Quality tab and find the cause.

Prevent it

  • Never leave the laser running.
  • Empty the crumb tray every day. Keep paper and scraps away.
  • Check the air assist before every job. Dirty optics and slow speeds cause flare-ups.

Other emergencies

8
Water leak or a water alarm.
Switch off the laser. Never run the tube without cooling water. Mop up, then check the hoses.
Run again only when the loop is full and clear.
9
Small burn on the skin.
Cool (not cold) running water for about 10 minutes (Mayo Clinic). Large or blistered burns: get medical help.
Restock the first aid kit.
10
