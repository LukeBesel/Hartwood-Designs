SOP3

Unload and pop-out check

When: Every job, before the sheets leave the laserTime: 2 to 4 min per job (Estimate)Who: Laser operator
You need:Bridge map for the sheet (kits/<kit>/popout/)A lamp or windowPlanner tablet
1
Before you lift: test a corner part.
Press lightly on a small part near each corner. It should move a little.
Moves: go on. Stuck: next step.
2
Stuck? Run the cut layer again.
Do not move the sheet. Turn off engrave and score. Run cut once.
Still stuck: weak power or dirty lens (SOP-5).
3
Lift the sheet flat. Hold it to the light.
Use both hands and do not bend it. Light should show along every cut line.
A dark spot in a line is an uncut spot. Log "uncut".
4
Compare with the bridge map.
Green dots show the bridges. Each part hangs by 1 to 4 bridges, 0.5 mm wide.
Blue edges slide into other parts: never a bridge there.
5
Pop test: first sheet of each batch.
Press from the back, right next to a bridge. One thumb should pop the part.
Put the test parts in the kit's small-parts bag.
6
Falls out alone, or tears when pushed?
Falls out: the kerf is too wide, lower cut power 5%. Tears or needs force: clean the lens, check focus.
Recut the coupon bridges (SOP-5). Log "bridge".
7
Half and quarter sheets: snap the tiles.
Bend gently along the long separation cut. Each tile goes to a different kit.
Clean edge, no part broken off at the line.
8
Check the show faces.
A light smoke stain sands off. A dark scorch on a visible part means remake the sheet.
Log "burn" if you remake it.
9
Stack flat by kit, with a label.
Label: kit, batch, date. Then mark the batch Cut in the planner.
Sheet letters match the job name.

Stop the batch and fix first if

  • Two or more uncut spots on one sheet, or parts falling out on two sheets in a row.
  • A crack runs through a part (bad sheet: set the stack of that lot aside).
