SOP2

Load and cut a job

When: Every laser job (two 12 x 12 sheets)Time: 2 to 3 min hands-on, then 15 to 25 min of attended cutting (Estimate)Who: Laser operator
You need:Planner tabletSheets for the jobStraight rulerHoneycomb pins
1
Pick the next job in the planner.
Cut queue lists the job file and the sheet colors. Oldest orders first.
Example job: dragon_job3_E-F_x4.
2
Check each sheet.
Lay it on the bench under a straight ruler. Color face up.
Bowed more than 1 mm (Estimate): use it for the coupon or scrap.
3
Open the job file.
<job>_bed.lbrn2 in the kit's cutfiles folder. The order is set: engrave, score, holes, outlines.
Speeds match the label on the machine.
4
Load two sheets.
First sheet letter on the left, in the corner stop. Second sheet beside it, 5 mm gap.
Flat on the bed. Pin a corner if it lifts.
5
Set the focus.
Autofocus, or enter 3 mm material height.
The nozzle does not touch the wood.
6
Frame the job.
Press Frame. The red dot traces the outline.
The outline stays on both sheets, with margin all round.
7
Close the lid and start.
Air assist and exhaust must be on.
Cut lines look thin and dark, not wide and black.
8
Stay and watch the whole job.
Never leave the room while the laser runs, not even for a minute.
A small flicker at the head is normal.
9
A flame stays after the head moves on? Press Pause.
Keep the lid shut. Most go out by themselves. If not: SOP-6.
Log it as a burn on the Quality tab.
10
When it ends, wait 30 seconds.
Let the exhaust clear the smoke before you open the lid.
Then unload with SOP-3.
11
UV-printed color: print first.
Print the front, white and back files from the kit's print_mode folder.
The print shows the 2 corner targets.
12
UV-printed color: Print and Cut.
In LightBurn, jog the red dot to target 1 and set it, then target 2, then align.
The cut lands on the print edge. The 0.6 mm bleed hides small errors.

Planning settings (Estimate until you cut the coupon, SOP-5)

  • Cut: 30 mm/s at 65% power. Score: 300 mm/s at 18%. Engrave: 400 mm/s at 20%, 0.1 mm line interval.
  • After the coupon, write your own numbers on a label on the machine: cut ____ mm/s ____% kerf ____ mm.

Hartwood Designs, SOP-2, Rev A, 2026-10-12. Times and settings marked Estimate are planning numbers until measured on the real machine. Machine details follow the OMTech manual when it differs from this sheet.
