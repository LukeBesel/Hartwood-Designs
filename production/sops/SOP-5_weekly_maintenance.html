SOP5

Weekly maintenance and calibration

When: Every week (Friday after the last job), after a tube change, and after moving the laserTime: About 45 min (Estimate)Who: Owner
You need:Lens tissue and lens cleanerBrush and vacuumDistilled waterFit coupon fileA scrap sheetThe log at the end of this SOP
1
Switch off and unplug.
Let the tube and head cool for 10 minutes.
Exhaust off, vent closed.
2
Clean the honeycomb and tray.
Lift out the honeycomb, brush off resin, empty and vacuum the tray under it.
No scraps left under the bed.
3
Clean mirrors 1, 2, 3 and the lens.
Lens cleaner on lens tissue, one wipe per tissue. Put each part back the same way round.
Cracked, pitted or brown: replace it.
4
Wipe the rails and belts.
Dry cloth on the rails. Oil only where and when the OMTech manual says.
The head slides smoothly by hand.
5
Check the water loop.
Level, clear water, no algae. Top up with distilled water. Change it as the manual says.
Above 10 °C. In winter keep the heat on.
6
Check air assist and exhaust.
Feel the air at the nozzle. Look in the duct for soot and grease. Clean the fan monthly.
Steady air. Duct clear.
7
Cut the fit coupon on this week's wood.
File fit_test_coupon in kits/coupon. Push a 3 mm strip into the slot comb. Note the snug slot.
Snug slot moved 0.1 mm or more: update the kerf.
8
Measure the kerf squares.
The square part should be 30.00 mm, the hole 20.00 mm. Also try the dowel holes and the 0.3 to 0.6 mm bridges.
Changed: set KERF in config.py, then run rebuild_all.sh.
9
Check the camera and frame.
Frame a shape on a scrap. The red dot should land where the camera image shows.
Off by more than 1 mm: recalibrate the camera in LightBurn.
10
Check the fire kit.
Extinguisher needle in green, pin and seal in place, blanket on its hook.
The smoke alarm beeps when tested.
