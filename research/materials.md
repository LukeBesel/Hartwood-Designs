# Materials: sheet size, thickness and colors

Checked 2026-10-11. See `sources.md` section S18 for links.

## Finding
- Natural 3 mm basswood plywood is sold in both **12 x 20 in** and **12 x 12 in**. **Verified**
- Pre-colored 3 mm basswood plywood (painted or acrylic-coated) is sold in **12 x 12 in** (or 300 x 300 mm), plus some 12 x 8 in and A4 packs. I found **no colored 12 x 20 in sheets**. **Verified** (absence in two searches; see S18)

## Decision: one sheet size = 12 x 12 in x 3 mm (nominal 305 x 305 mm)
- We design for the smallest real size seen, **300 x 300 mm**, with an 8 mm margin, so the nest area is **284 x 284 mm**.
- 12 x 20 in also fits the Polar 2 bed (508 x 305 mm inside 710 x 360 mm), but colors only come in 12 x 12, so 12 x 12 is the only size that works for everything.
- Bonus: two 12 x 12 sheets fit side by side on the Polar 2 (610 mm of 710 mm). One door opening = two sheets.

## Thickness
All parts are designed for **3.0 mm nominal**. Real plywood runs 2.8 to 3.2 mm. Every slot width is a parameter, set from the fit-test coupon for each new batch of wood.
