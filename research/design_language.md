# Design language teardown: what makes a kit feel like ROKR

Checked 2026-10-11. Words only, with links. I did not download, copy or reuse any ROKR images, text, instructions or designs. Hartwood kits use their own shapes, names, art and booklet layout.

Labels: **Verified** = a source says it (see `sources.md`). **Observed pattern (Estimate)** = my summary across many listings.

## 1. Material and color
- Natural light plywood (basswood or similar) is the base color of almost every kit. **Verified** ("pre-cut from non-toxic, environmentally friendly plywood", [manuals.plus](https://manuals.plus/asin/B08ML5XBL1))
- Laser-burned brown edges are part of the look. Every cut edge is dark, so layered parts read as outlined drawings. **Observed pattern (Estimate)**
- Color is used as small accents, not big painted areas. ROKR sells "colorful wood" versions of some kits (Cuckoo Clock "Colorful Wood" option). **Verified** ([robotimeonline Cuckoo Clock](https://www.robotimeonline.com/products/rokr-cuckoo-clock-mechanical-3d-wooden-puzzle))
- Rowood flowers use colored sheets for petals and leaves. **Verified** ([Rowood bouquet](https://www.robotimeonline.com/products/rowood-wooden-flower-bouquet-3d-wooden-puzzle-tw01h))

**Hartwood rule:** natural basswood body + 1 to 3 accent colors from pre-colored sheets. Never painted parts.

## 2. Engraved surface detail
- Part numbers are burned onto the part itself. **Verified** ([The Gadgeteer review](https://the-gadgeteer.com/2025/05/07/rokr-dream-gift-factory-wooden-music-box-kit-review/))
- Surface detail (panel lines, rivets, wood grain, scale patterns) is laser engraved or scored so a flat plate looks like a machined part. **Observed pattern (Estimate)**
- Dark text on a dark sheet is hard to read. **Verified complaint** ([manuals.plus guide](https://manuals.plus/video/a3dc6d5d481dd45a26db69a34a749595560a29f303335886355c428abe3dba63))

**Hartwood rule:** engrave detail only where it reads from 1 m away (eyes, scales, veins, panel lines). Use thin "score" lines, not big filled areas, to save laser time. Part codes engraved when they fit; on pre-painted sheets the engraving removes paint and shows light wood, which is easy to read.

## 3. Layered parts
- Depth comes from stacking flat parts: 2 to 5 layers make a curved-looking body, a thick gear hub or a raised panel. **Observed pattern (Estimate)**
- Flowers use several petal layers rotated against each other to fake a round bloom. **Verified** (about 100 pieces per flower in the Rowood bouquet, [rokronline](https://www.rokronline.com/products/rowood-diy-wooden-flower-bouquet-3d-wooden-puzzle))

**Hartwood rule:** stacks of slices slide down over a square key or peg, so layers cannot spin or slip.

## 4. Glue-free joints
- Mortise and tenon (tab into slot) is the main joint. **Verified** ("mortise and tenon joint system ... without the need for glue", [Gear Ball Challenge](https://www.robotimeonline.com/products/rokr-gear-ball-challenge-3d-wooden-puzzle-egc01))
- Wax stick for gears and tabs; sandpaper in the box. **Verified** ([manuals.plus ROKR](https://manuals.plus/category/rokr))
- Some kits add dowels, metal rods and small screws. **Verified** ([Marble Spaceport review](https://www.robotimeonline.com/blogs/all-blogs/rokr-marble-spaceport-marble-run-building-review))

**Hartwood rule:** four joint types only (tab in square hole, cross half-lap, slices on a key plate, discs on a square peg). Dowels only as axles.

## 5. How parts stay in the sheet
- Parts are held by tiny bridges ("nibs"). You press around the edge to break them. Pushing from the back reduces splintering. **Verified** ([manuals.plus removal video](https://manuals.plus/video/a3dc6d5d481dd45a26db69a34a749595560a29f303335886355c428abe3dba63))
- Sheets are labeled with letters, parts with numbers, and both match the booklet. **Verified** (same)
- A small push stick is often included. **Verified** (same)

**Hartwood rule:** 0.6 mm bridges, never on a mating face. Sheet letter + part number on every part (for example `B12`). Sheets are flat, stack in the box, no sorting.

## 6. Packaging and box art
- Box shows the finished model on a plain background, plus piece count, build time, difficulty and age 14+. **Observed pattern (Estimate)** from product listing titles that repeat these facts (for example "324 PCS, 12H Assembly", [Amazon Typewriter](https://us.amazon.com/ROKR-Mechanical-Typewriter-Assembly-Building/dp/B0GLX17X24))
- Inside: wooden sheets, booklet, small tools (sandpaper, wax, push stick, sometimes screwdriver). **Verified** ([manuals.plus guide](https://manuals.plus/video/a3dc6d5d481dd45a26db69a34a749595560a29f303335886355c428abe3dba63))

**Hartwood rule:** kraft box, one big hero render, 4 icon badges (pieces, hours, difficulty dots, 14+). Sheets stack flat; hardware in one small bag.

## 7. Instruction booklet style
- "Almost completely relies on images"; areas color-coded; screw sizes printed 1:1; parts list in the left column of each step; 5 languages. **Verified** ([The Gadgeteer](https://the-gadgeteer.com/2025/05/07/rokr-dream-gift-factory-wooden-music-box-kit-review/))
- Parts list grouped by bag with 1:1 drawings of rods and dowels. **Verified** ([Marble Spaceport review](https://www.robotimeonline.com/blogs/all-blogs/rokr-marble-spaceport-marble-run-building-review))
- Typical step: 2 to 8 new parts, shown in an isometric drawing, with part codes and arrows. **Observed pattern (Estimate)**

**Hartwood rule:** one step = max 6 new parts. New parts in color, built parts in light gray, codes with "x2" counts, arrows for insertion, icons for "flip", "press", "do not glue", "test it now". QR code per step into the 3D app.

## What Hartwood does differently (on purpose)
1. Our own shapes and names. No replica firearms; our blaster is an original toy design.
2. A "test it now" check after every mechanism step (reviews say mechanisms fail and builders only find out at the end).
3. Free replacement of any part by code (we cut on demand, so this is cheap for us).
4. Spares of small or fragile parts printed on the sheet in leftover space.
5. A 3D app for every step (ROKR uses videos and paper only).
