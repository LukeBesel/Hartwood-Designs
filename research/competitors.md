## Themes that matter for our three kits
- **Blasters:** ROKR replicas of real firearms at $27 to $66; Wooden-City (wooden-town.com) AK-style at $99 and a toy revolver at $69. **Verified**. Nobody in the sample sells an original, clearly-toy, sci-fi or steampunk wooden blaster at a mid price. Etsy bans realistic replicas even with an orange tip; brightly colored toy guns may be allowed. **Verified** ([Etsy weapons policy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/policy/weapons/1354773824894))
- **Dragons:** Ugears Wood Dragon 141 pcs $24.99 (easy, Chinese-style), Ugears Windstorm Dragon $61.99 (rubber band motor), KCO Dragon 320 pcs $74 (paper wings), Dragon-vibe 145 pcs $29 (static), Wood Trick Dragon Castle $149.95. **Verified**. ROKR has none. Gap: a colorful, ROKR-style display dragon with engraved scales and a scene base at $45 to $55.
- **Flowers:** Rowood bouquet 581 pcs $39.99, Ugears Mechanical Flower 101 pcs $55.99. **Verified**. Music box + flowers + pastel colors only partly served (ROKR Secret Garden AMK52, $36.99). Gap: a pastel flower music box gift at $40 to $50.
