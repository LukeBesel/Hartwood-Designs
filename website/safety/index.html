Safety

Our kits are hobby models for ages 14 and up. Please read this before you build.

For every kit

  • Small parts. Keep loose parts away from young children and pets.
  • Laser-cut edges can be slightly sharp or sooty. Wipe them with a dry cloth if you like.
  • Do not force parts. If one is tight, sand the tab a little. Thin parts can snap.
  • Wood burns. Keep the finished model away from candles, heaters and stoves.

Star Slinger (rubber band shooter)

  • Wear glasses when you fire it. Bands can bounce back.
  • Aim only at the paper target. Never at faces, people or animals.
  • Use #64 rubber bands only, at most 7 at a time.
  • The blaze orange nose, plug and cap are part of the ship. They show it is a toy. Do not remove or paint them.
  • Do not carry it in public places where someone could mistake it for something else.

Blossom Waltz (music box)

  • Do not over-wind the key. Stop when it gets firm.

Questions about safety? Contact us.
