How it works

  1. 1. Push out

    Every part is cut and coded. Press it out from the back of the sheet.

  2. 2. Build

    Follow the picture booklet, or turn each step around in 3D on your phone.

  3. 3. Make it move

    Test the moving parts as you go. Then set it on a shelf and show it off.

Instructions you can turn around

Scan the code on any page of the booklet. The same step opens in 3D: new parts glow orange and slide into place. Spin the model with a finger, tap any part to see its code and sheet, and pick up where you left off. It even works without internet after the first visit.

Good to know

Do I need glue or tools?

No. Parts press together. A wax stick and sandpaper are in the box for moving parts and tight spots.

Who are the kits for?

Ages 14 and up. Many adults build them as a relaxing evening project or as a gift.

What if I lose a part?

Many small parts have a spare on the sheet. For anything else, send us the part code and we will mail one free.
